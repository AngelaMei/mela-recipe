## 三杯雞
*Three-cup chicken*
Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=abc123
Note: 媽媽的做法，九層塔要最後放

### 食材
- 雞腿 — 600g
- 大蒜 — 10瓣
- 薑 — 1塊
- 九層塔 — 一把
- 麻油 — 3大匙
- 醬油 — 3大匙
- 米酒 — 1杯
- 冰糖 — 1大匙

### 步驟
1. 雞腿切塊，用滾水汆燙後瀝乾
2. 麻油小火煸香薑片和大蒜
3. 下雞腿炒到表面金黃
4. 加醬油、米酒、冰糖，蓋鍋燜 15 分鐘
5. 收汁後加入九層塔拌勻

## Shakshuka
*番茄燉蛋*

### Ingredients
- Eggs — 4
- Tomatoes — 800g
- Onion — 1
- Garlic — 3 cloves
- Cumin — 1 tsp
- Feta — to taste
- Olive oil 2 tbsp

### Steps
1. Soften the onion in olive oil, then add the garlic and cumin
2. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes
3. Make wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook until just set
4. Crumble feta on top and serve with bread
